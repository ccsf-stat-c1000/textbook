When Is It Safe to Use These Methods?¶

As we mentioned before, one of the most important things to learn with any inference method is the conditions under which it is safe to use it.

As we did for the mean, the assumption we made in order to develop the methods in this unit was that the sampling distribution of the sample proportion, p^\hat{p}p^​, is roughly normal.
